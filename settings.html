Shifts the song's pitch live (tempo unchanged) so your guitar can stay in its tuning. Use the TUNE control in the player's plugin controls. Note detection and the highway follow the shifted tuning. Your song files are never changed.

Eb Standard songs move up to E Standard, Drop C# to Drop D, and so on. A shift you set by hand for a song always wins.

Player shortcuts: , / . semitone down / up, < / > 5 cents, \ back to auto.
